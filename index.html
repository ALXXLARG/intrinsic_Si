The key idea: a hole is an absence of an electron.

When an electron in a neighboring covalent bond moves into the missing-electron state, it leaves a new vacancy in its former bond. Therefore the hole appears to move opposite to the electron hop. The promoted conduction electron is shown separately in the top band.

Electron: neighbor → vacancy ⇄ Hole: vacancy → neighbor
Teaching-model note. Silicon has a 3D tetrahedral crystal structure. This 2D bond-site lattice is a simplified visualization; each line represents a covalent bond containing a shared electron pair. The speed slider changes display speed only, not physical carrier mobility. With no applied electric field, thermal motion has no preferred net direction.
